SleepGuardian — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SleepGuardian is a two-part app (a Wear OS watch app and a companion phone app) that detects when you fall asleep and automatically turns off your TV. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is stored, and what (if anything) ever leaves your devices.

The short version

  • All of your data stays on your own watch and phone. There is no user account, no cloud database, and no server operated by us that stores your personal information.
  • The app contains no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • A small amount of technical diagnostic data (not your health data) is sent to a developer debugging tool — details below.

Data used on your devices

To do its job, the app reads and stores the following data locally, using Android's standard on-device storage (DataStore). None of it is uploaded to any account or cloud service:

Technical diagnostic logs

To debug issues without needing physical access to a tester's devices during development, the app sends small, best-effort diagnostic events (e.g. "phone received a trigger from the watch", "TV reconnect succeeded", current battery percentage) to a webhook run on Tines, a workflow-automation service used here purely as a developer debugging log.

Each diagnostic event contains only:

  • which part of the app sent it (watch or phone)
  • a short event name and human-readable technical detail (e.g. sensor status, connection outcome)
  • a timestamp

These events never include your actual heart rate readings, your name, your Google account, your location, or any advertising identifier. As with any network request, the sending device's IP address is visible to that service, the same as it would be to any website you visit.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately, since nothing is kept anywhere else. There is no account to delete because none is ever created.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Since the app has no accounts, we cannot notify you individually — please check back here if you have concerns.
