SleepGuardian — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SleepGuardian is a two-part app (a Wear OS watch app and a companion phone app) that detects when you fall asleep and automatically turns off your TV. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is stored, and what (if anything) ever leaves your devices.

The short version

  • Your heart rate, movement data, sleep schedule and TV details stay on your own watch and phone. There is no user account, no cloud database, and no server operated by us that stores your personal information.
  • The app contains no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • A small amount of technical diagnostic data (never your heart-rate readings) is sent to a developer debugging tool — details below.

To do its job, the app reads and stores the following data locally, using Android's standard on-device storage (DataStore). None of it is uploaded to any account or cloud service:

The watch and phone talk to each other over Bluetooth through Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer. These messages only carry the "sleep detected" trigger, setup and connection checks, and the TV result — no heart-rate or movement data.

Technical diagnostic logs

To debug issues without needing physical access to a tester's devices during development, the app sends small, best-effort diagnostic events (e.g. "phone received a trigger from the watch", "TV reconnect succeeded", current battery percentage) to a webhook run on Tines, a workflow-automation service used here purely as a developer debugging log.

Each diagnostic event contains only:

  • which part of the app sent it (watch or phone) and the app's version number
  • a short event name and human-readable technical detail (e.g. sensor status, connection outcome, or an error message, which can include the local network address of your TV or phone)
  • a timestamp
  • for monitoring summaries: counts and percentages only (for example how many heart-rate readings arrived, what share of the time your heart rate was below the detection threshold, and what share of the time you were still), plus battery level and power-saving state
  • if the app crashes: a technical error report (stack trace)
  • your device model and Android version, which are sent automatically with every web request

These events never include your actual heart rate readings, your name, your Google account, your location, or any advertising identifier, and they are not linked to an account. As with any network request, the sending device's IP address is visible to that service, the same as it would be to any website you visit. The events are read only by the app's developer and are not shared with anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately, since nothing is kept anywhere else. There is no account to delete because none is ever created. Diagnostic events already received by the developer's debugging tool are not tied to your name or an account; if you would like them removed, email the address below with your device model and the approximate date, and we will do our best to find and delete them.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Since the app has no accounts, we cannot notify you individually — please check back here if you have concerns.
